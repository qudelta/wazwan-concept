Business is business · 06
I don’t mind people negotiating. But it gets weird when your relationship becomes the discount.

You don’t get cheaper electricity because you know the owner.

You don’t get cheaper fuel because you’re friends with the pump guy.

You don’t negotiate with Apple because you went to school with Tim Cook.

So why does knowing me suddenly make my work worth less?
